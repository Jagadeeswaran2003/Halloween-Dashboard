# Halloween Dashboard
This Halloween event dashboard analyzes participant trends from 2008 to 2014 and forecasts future attendance. The heatmap reveals peak participation times, with 8:15 PM consistently drawing the largest crowds—up to 869 participants in 2011. Visitor numbers grew steadily from 2008 to 2011 but began to decline after 2012, dropping to 391 in 2013 before a slight recovery. The forecast section predicts a stable turnout of 657 participants from 2014 to 2016. Year-over-year variation shows a spike in total participation in 2011 (869), indicating a particularly successful event. The bar chart highlights 8 PM and 8:15 PM as the strongest time slots, with visitor counts of 4,004 and 4,147, respectively. Participation at 6 PM is consistently zero, suggesting the event gains momentum later in the evening. The event appears to be most successful between 7:30 PM and 8:15 PM, and planning should focus on these time windows. Overall, the dashboard suggests a decline after 2011 but also stability and potential for recovery.
